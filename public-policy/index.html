Policy brief

Governing AI in Commercial Gaming

A risk-based supervisory approach to player protection

Artificial intelligence now decides which promotion a customer receives, when a session is interrupted, and whether someone is flagged as at risk — while the rules governing those decisions were written for static systems configured by hand. The brief proposes classifying operator AI by its capacity to affect customers, and scaling obligations accordingly.
